Privacy statement
Local calculator mode
Buckets can be used without an account. In this mode, your plan, saved scenarios, statements, and transactions stay in this browser unless you choose to export a file.
Statement audit
When you use the audit tools, PDF statements are read on your device by the browser. Buckets uses that information to help you review transactions without sending the statement file to a Buckets server. Private audit mode keeps itemized audit rows out of autosave and exported plan files.
Local learning
Auto-categorize can learn from corrections only when you click Learn on a reviewed audit row. Buckets stores cleaned merchant/category hints in this browser. It does not upload transactions or use a cloud AI service in this local calculator mode, and you can clear the learning from the Audit tab.
Optional cloud features
If Buckets adds accounts or cloud storage in the future, those features will be optional and clearly labeled before any data is stored outside your device.
Planning disclaimer
Buckets is an educational planning calculator, not financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

How buckets work
Buckets are the places your monthly money is assigned. This is a simple overview; click Details on a bucket for the more specific type options and fields.
Spending
Everyday categories like rent, food, insurance, fun, travel, or misc. These usually do not need a balance.
Savings and goals
Use for emergency fund, cottage, wedding, or other target balances. Specific types may include Savings goal or Goal bucket.
Debt payoff
Monthly money in these buckets is pooled for debt payoff. Debt balances and interest rates live in Your situation or Advanced debt.
Investment and retirement
Use for 401(k), retirement, brokerage, HSA, or personal investing. Details can store growth assumptions and employer match when relevant.
Wellness
A gentle category for health, self-care, fitness, therapy, or restorative spending. Audit review can celebrate these when selected.
