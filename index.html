Privacy statement
Local calculator mode
Realm can be used without an account. In this mode, your plan, saved scenarios, statements, and transactions stay in this browser unless you choose to export a file.
Statement audit
When you use the audit tools, PDF statements are read on your device by the browser. Realm uses that information to help you review transactions without sending the statement file to a Realm server.
Optional cloud features
If Realm adds accounts or cloud storage in the future, those features will be optional and clearly labeled before any data is stored outside your device.
Planning disclaimer
Realm is an educational planning calculator, not financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.
